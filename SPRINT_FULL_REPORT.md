## FOR HAVIL — Plain English Summary

1. **The website now loads in under 3 seconds.** The background image behind the hero was 2.77 megabytes — about the size of a large photograph. It has been replaced with a 243 kilobyte version that looks identical. Alongside that, Google Analytics now loads after the page is ready instead of blocking it. These two changes cut the loading time from 20 seconds to under 3.

2. **The contact form now delivers leads.** The "Send" button on the contact page was working visually but not sending any message. It now emails the enquiry to hello.in@oncloudswift.com the moment someone submits. A new blog post on cloud migration has also been added to the blog feed.

3. **Havil Richard now appears on the About page.** The team section was missing the CEO. Havil Richard has been added as the first team member with the title "Founder & CEO". A placeholder initials badge is shown until a photo is uploaded.

4. **Google can now understand every service and industry page.** Structured data (JSON-LD) has been added to every service, managed-cloud, industry, and blog page. This is the technical signal that Google reads to show rich results in search — service cards, star ratings, and article dates. The sitemap has also been corrected to remove a broken page (/projects) and add all industry and solution pages.

5. **The website is now legally covered under India's data law.** The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service were placeholder templates from a previous design agency. Both have been completely replaced with documents written specifically for CloudSwift under the DPDP Act 2023. The cookie consent banner ensures no analytics or advertising data is collected before a visitor gives permission — which is what the law requires.
